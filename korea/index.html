Frequently Asked Questions

Do your drivers in Korea speak English?

Yes, English-speaking drivers are available on request. Just let us know when you submit your request and we'll confirm availability before booking.

Which airports do you cover?

Incheon International Airport (ICN) and Gimpo International Airport (GMP), covering both international and domestic arrivals.

Can I book a private tour without a fixed itinerary?

Yes. Tell us how many hours you'd like and the kind of places you're interested in — palaces, markets, cafes, nature — and we'll build the day around you.

Can I travel to other cities from Seoul?

Yes, intercity transfers are available, including Seoul to Busan, or day trips to the DMZ, Nami Island, or Everland.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 2-3 days ahead. Same-day requests are handled case by case depending on vehicle availability.

What vehicle sizes are available?

Sedan (1-3 passengers), SUV or minivan (4-7 passengers), and van for larger groups (8+).
